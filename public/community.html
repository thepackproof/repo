Community Standards

PackProof is private, but uploaded content and interactions must still be safe and lawful.

Do not upload or transact

  • Illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled or unlawfully regulated items.
  • Weapons, drugs, sexual exploitation material or instructions facilitating serious harm.
  • Government IDs, full payment-card numbers, account credentials or unrelated private communications.
  • Media captured without legally required permission.
  • Threats, harassment, hate, impersonation, scams or attempts to manipulate evidence.
  • Content infringing intellectual-property or privacy rights.

Reporting and blocking

Open the relevant PackProof, choose “Raise a concern,” select a reason and describe the issue. You can also block the other participant from future transactions. Reports are reviewed according to severity and may result in content restriction, account suspension, preservation for legal purposes or reporting to appropriate authorities.

Appeals

Send moderation appeals to nericollin@thepackproof.com with the PackProof transaction ID and the reason you believe the decision should be reconsidered. Do not email evidence files containing sensitive personal information.
