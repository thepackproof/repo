PACKPROOF

Neutral evidence infrastructure for e-commerce.

PackProof creates a shared, private, review-ready record connecting item details, mutually confirmed terms, packing, arrival, unboxing, and returns.

A structured record from agreement through arrival

Guided packing and unboxing, a visible human-reviewable package-seal protocol, server receipt and finalization times, create-restricted evidence records, SHA-256 file fingerprints, and an exportable timeline help both participants preserve and retrieve what they submitted and confirmed.

PackProof records and organizes evidence. It does not authenticate an item or person, prove scene truth or custody, determine fraud or fault, provide escrow or insurance, decide disputes, or guarantee acceptance or outcomes.
