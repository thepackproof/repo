Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Controller / Developer: PackProof LLC (“PackProof,” “we,” “us,” or “our”)

This Privacy Policy explains how PackProof collects, uses, discloses, protects, retains, and deletes personal information when you use the PackProof mobile application, websites, participant portals, and consumer-facing services that link to this policy (collectively, the “Service”). It also explains choices and rights that may apply to you.

This policy is designed for the current U.S. closed-beta configuration. If PackProof enables new sign-in providers, advertising, paid subscriptions, analytics products, international availability, or materially different data processing, we will update this policy and associated platform disclosures before or when those changes take effect as required by law.

1. Summary of Our Privacy Approach

  • PackProof collects transaction evidence and related metadata because evidence preservation is the core function of the Service.
  • Optional precise location is off by default and is not required to use the core Service.
  • PackProof does not sell personal information for money and does not use transaction evidence for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.
  • PackProof does not receive or store your fingerprint template, face template, or device passcode when the app invokes operating-system biometric or device authentication.
  • Transaction content is shared with authorized transaction participants and service providers as necessary to operate the Service.
  • You can request account deletion in the app and through PackProof’s web deletion process. Certain narrow records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute preservation, or rights of another transaction participant, as explained below.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

We may collect your name, display name, email address, profile image, internal account identifiers, sign-in provider identifiers, account status, acceptance records for legal terms, and authentication or security events. If you use Google sign-in, PackProof receives information authorized through that sign-in flow; PackProof does not receive your Google password.

2.2 Transaction and Commerce Information

We collect information users submit or import about a transaction, which may include item description, condition, price or transaction value, SKU or variant information, serial or certificate information, order or receipt identifiers, transaction terms, participant roles and confirmations, carrier, tracking number, shipment status, return information, concern or claim context, and timestamps. Imported receipt, email, or commerce data may be incomplete or unverified unless the Service expressly describes a specific verification step.

2.3 Evidence and User Content

We collect photos, videos, audio included in evidence videos, documents, reports, text submitted through forms, and other files you choose or are asked to submit. Evidence may include the item, packaging, seal, shipping label, barcode, package condition, unpacking, return packing, or related transaction context.

2.4 Evidence Metadata and Integrity Information

We collect or generate metadata associated with evidence, such as capture and upload times, file size, media type, image or video dimensions, uploader and participant role, transaction association, camera-read barcode or tracking context, cryptographic hashes, manifest identifiers, app version, capture-profile information, queue status, server event history, and integrity or limitation states. The Service may also record device/app-attestation context, a device-key or signature result, and limited sensor or motion statistics used to characterize capture context.

2.5 Device, App, Network, and Diagnostic Information

We may collect device manufacturer and model, operating-system version, app version, app installation or integrity information, device capabilities relevant to evidence capture, language or locale, network-connection type, notification token, error information, and diagnostic logs. The Service may create or receive privacy-preserving security values derived from network context for abuse prevention. We do not use Android advertising identifiers for the initial release configuration described by this policy.

2.6 Optional Precise Location

If you affirmatively enable optional location for a capture and grant device permission, PackProof may collect precise latitude/longitude, accuracy, altitude when available, and associated capture time. Location is off by default, is not required for ordinary evidence capture, and is treated as sensitive information. We use it only for the user-authorized transaction-evidence context, security, integrity review, and related service functions described at the time of collection.

2.7 Local Biometric / Device Authentication

PackProof may ask Android or another operating system to authenticate you using a fingerprint, face recognition, device credential, or other local authentication method supported by your device. The operating system performs that authentication. PackProof does not receive or store your biometric template, fingerprint image, face geometry, or device passcode. PackProof may receive only the authentication result or related non-biometric status necessary to continue the requested action.

2.8 Support, Safety, and Communications

If you contact support, report a concern, participate in beta feedback, or communicate with PackProof, we collect the information you provide, relevant account or transaction identifiers, attachments, and records reasonably necessary to respond, investigate abuse, or maintain support history.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you, when you create an account, enter transaction terms, capture evidence, import or share documents, submit forms, or contact support.
  • From another authorized transaction participant, when that participant creates or updates a shared transaction record or submits evidence visible to you.
  • Automatically from the app, device, and our systems when necessary for authentication, security, capture, upload, finalization, notifications, diagnostics, and workflow operation.
  • From service providers and integrations, such as sign-in providers, cloud infrastructure, carriers, commerce platforms, or receipt/order sources, when you or an authorized partner initiates or permits the integration.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain user accounts and authenticate access.
  • Create, manage, and present shared transaction records and authorized participant workflows.
  • Capture, encrypt, upload, store, finalize, hash, organize, and present transaction evidence.
  • Generate Proofs, reports, timelines, exports, still frames, thumbnails, or other presentation derivatives.
  • Operate invitations, handoffs, app links, notifications, and participant communications.
  • Detect unauthorized access, tampering, replay, abuse, fraud, malware, suspicious activity, and security incidents.
  • Troubleshoot failures, measure reliability, test beta features, improve usability, and maintain compatible devices and integrations.
  • Respond to support requests, rights requests, account deletion, complaints, disputes, and legal process.
  • Enforce our Terms, protect users and third parties, establish or defend legal claims, and comply with applicable law.
  • Perform other purposes disclosed to you at collection or with your consent where required.

Information associated with a shared transaction may be disclosed to the other authorized participant or participants according to their role and the Service’s access controls. This can include transaction terms, participant confirmations, evidence, timestamps, status, and presentation records. Precise location, where collected, should remain limited to the private authorized evidence context and is not intended for public reports.

5.2 Service Providers

We disclose information to vendors and infrastructure providers that process data for us or support the Service, which may include Google Firebase / Google Cloud services, Google sign-in, Google Play distribution and integrity services, Expo-related build/update infrastructure, email or notification providers, hosting providers, security providers, and other vendors necessary to operate the Service. These providers may process data under their own terms and data-processing commitments.

5.3 Integrations You or an Authorized Partner Request

If you use or authorize a carrier, commerce, claims, receipt, email-import, API, or other integration, information may be exchanged with that integration as necessary to provide the requested functionality. Separate partner terms or privacy disclosures may apply to enterprise or API implementations.

5.4 Legal, Safety, and Rights Protection

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to comply with law, subpoena, court order, or lawful government request; investigate fraud or security incidents; prevent imminent harm; enforce agreements; or establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

5.5 Corporate Transactions

Information may be disclosed as part of due diligence or transferred in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of PackProof’s business or assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

5.6 No Sale or Targeted Advertising

PackProof does not sell personal information for money. In the current U.S. closed-beta configuration, PackProof does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not process personal information for targeted advertising as those concepts are defined by applicable U.S. state privacy laws. We also do not use transaction evidence to build advertising profiles. If those practices materially change, we will update this policy and provide legally required choices before or when the change takes effect.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, the period communicated in the product, or as required or permitted by law. Retention depends on the data category, transaction status, account status, security needs, dispute or legal-hold requirements, and whether another authorized participant has an interest in a shared record.

Data categoryGeneral retention approach
Account/profile informationRetained while the account is active and thereafter only as needed to complete deletion, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, financial-record, or legal obligations.
Transaction records and participant timelineRetained for the operational transaction-retention period and as reasonably necessary to preserve the integrity of a shared record, resolve disputes, comply with law, or protect another participant’s legitimate record. Account deletion may result in de-identification or redaction of the departing participant from portions of a shared timeline rather than silently rewriting the remaining participant’s historical record.
Evidence files and derivativesRetained for the applicable evidence/transaction retention period, subject to deletion requests, legal hold, security, or dispute-preservation requirements. Local unsynchronized evidence may be lost if device-bound keys or app data become unavailable.
Security, integrity, and audit recordsRetained for periods reasonably necessary to detect abuse, investigate incidents, prevent replay or fraud, demonstrate system integrity, enforce agreements, and comply with law. These records may outlast ordinary account content where justified.
Support and rights-request recordsRetained long enough to resolve the request, document our response, prevent abuse, and meet legal or recordkeeping obligations.
Backups and disaster-recovery copiesMay persist for a limited rolling period after deletion until overwritten or expired under backup schedules, with access restricted to recovery, security, and legal purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion through the in-app account settings and through PackProof’s designated web deletion resource. We will take reasonable steps to verify that the requester controls the account and to protect against unauthorized deletion. We will not require unnecessary steps that make deletion materially harder than account creation or ordinary use.

When an account-deletion request becomes final, PackProof will delete or de-identify personal information associated with the account that we are not required or reasonably permitted to retain. We may retain limited information where necessary for security or fraud prevention, legal compliance, legal holds, dispute preservation, exercise or defense of legal claims, financial or tax obligations, or to preserve the integrity of another participant’s shared transaction history. Where feasible, retained shared history will be minimized or de-identified rather than continuing to expose a deleted user’s profile information.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove information from encrypted backups or disaster-recovery media; those copies are isolated from ordinary use and expire or are overwritten under normal backup schedules unless preservation is legally required.

8. Security

PackProof uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed for the nature of the information we process. Current controls may include authenticated access, server-authoritative workflow changes, private cloud storage rules, encryption in transit, provider-managed encryption at rest, Android Keystore protection for certain queued offline evidence, app-attestation services, create-only or restricted evidence upload paths, cryptographic hashes and manifest binding, rate limits, audit records, and least-privilege access controls.

No service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device and linked accounts, installing security updates, and promptly reporting suspected compromise. If you lose a device or uninstall/clear the app before queued evidence synchronizes, device-bound encrypted evidence may become unrecoverable.

9. Your Choices

  • Notifications: you can manage notification permission in device settings, although disabling notifications may cause you to miss transaction updates.
  • Camera/microphone: evidence capture requires the permissions presented for the relevant feature. You can deny permission, but the associated capture feature may not function.
  • Account export and deletion: use available account tools or contact us using the information below.
  • Communications: service and security communications may be necessary to operate your account. Marketing communications, if introduced, will include legally required choices.
